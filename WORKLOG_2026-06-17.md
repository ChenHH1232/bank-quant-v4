## 4. Data Integrity Findings

### Quarterly statement tail

- All 42 banks are missing:
  - `2026q2`
  - `2026q3`
  - `2026q4`
- This is treated as expected because the current raw window ends at `2026-05-01`.

### Annual bank indicator gap

- `bank_indicator` is systematically unavailable for:
  - `2024`
  - `2025`
  - `2026`
- This is treated as provider-side unavailability rather than local download failure.

### Ordinary vs financial-company statement overlap

- Confirmed on Ping An Bank and random 10-bank sample:
  - ordinary `income` vs financial-company `finance_income_statement_current_only` overlap heavily
  - ordinary `cash_flow` vs financial-company `finance_cashflow_statement_current_only` overlap heavily
  - ordinary `balance` vs `finance_balance_sheet_parent_current_only` overlap heavily
- Main interpretation:
  - income / cash-flow overlap is mainly a `single-quarter vs cumulative-to-date` issue
  - balance overlap is mainly a `general / consolidated-like vs parent-scope` issue
